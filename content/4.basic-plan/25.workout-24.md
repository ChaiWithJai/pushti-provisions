# Workout 24

## Dynamic warm-up

1. **Fighting stance drill** — 6 rounds of 1 minute, with 20 seconds of rest. Throw a basic punch, then throw it as a counter.
2. **Chair drill** — 4 rounds of 2 minutes. Sit on a chair, get up and throw 2 punches, with steps forward.
3. **Defensive drill** — slip – pull back – slip – roll – roll.
4. **Fighting stance ladder drills** — 3 rounds of 2 minutes. Move and throw punch combos: 1 — forward/backward, 2 — to the sides, 3 — pivots, shifts and sidesteps.

## Virtual sparring against a short guy — 5 rounds of 3 minutes

- Only lead hand
- Lead hand + 1 rear hand punch per attack
- Freestyle
- Change stance

## Finisher

- Drills with resistance bands — 1 round of 3 minutes, in the opposite stance.
- Agility footwork drill — 2 rounds.

Finish with stretches.
