# Workout 5

## Dynamic warm-up

1. **Fighting stance drill** — 4 rounds of 1 minute. Touch your knee and throw a punch.

*10 jump squats.*

## Shadow boxing — 5 rounds of 3 minutes

1. Only movement
2. Only long range attacks
3. Only close range attacks
4. Freestyle
5. Change the stance

## Virtual defensive drill

1 round of 3 minutes. "I throw single punches, you block and dodge them." (Round 1 of the linked video.)

## Bag work — 6 rounds of 3 minutes

1. Only movement
2. Only long range attacks
3. Only close range attacks
4. Freestyle
5. Change the stance
6. Conditioning bag work drill (1 round)

## Finisher

- Tennis ball to the wall drill — 6 rounds of 1 minute with 20 seconds of rest.
- Shadow boxing with resistance bands — 6 rounds of 1 minute with 20 seconds of rest.
- 1 set of 80–90% of max reps, with 1 minute of rest between the exercises:
  - Knuckle push-ups
  - Squats with a med ball throw
  - Pull-ups
  - Sit-ups with med ball throws

Finish with stretches.
