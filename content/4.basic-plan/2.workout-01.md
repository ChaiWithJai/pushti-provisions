# Workout 1

## Dynamic warm-up

1. **Frontal stance drill** — 4 rounds of 2 minutes with 30 seconds of rest in between.
2. **Fighting stance drill** — 4 rounds of 2 minutes. Swing like a tree: rotate your shoulders and start throwing punches (rear hand forward, lead hand backward).

*20 knuckle push-ups, 20 mountain climbers.*

3. **Frontal stance movement drill** — 1 round of 2 minutes. Just go in all directions.
4. **Fighting stance movement drill** — 1 round of 2 minutes. Just go in all directions, use Z and L steps.

*12 jump squats.*

5. **Frontal stance defensive drill** — 1 round of 2 minutes. Slip, slip, move, roll, roll.
6. **Fighting stance defensive drill** — 1 round of 2 minutes. Slip, slip, move, roll, roll.

*10 burpees.*

## Bag work — 6 rounds of 3 minutes

1. Movement drill with a bag
2. Approach — throw any single punch — exit
3. Approach — throw any 2 punches — exit
4. Defensive drill
5. Any punch — defend the same punch
6. Conditioning drill (below)

## Conditioning drill

Medicine ball throws (straight punches, to the sides, down and up) — 6–8 reps for each exercise.

## Finisher

- Knuckle push-ups, squats, balanced tucks — 30 seconds each without rest, then 1 minute of rest.
- Plyometric push-ups (8–12), jump squats (8–12), hanging leg raises (max).

Finish with stretches.
