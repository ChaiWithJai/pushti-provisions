# Workout 6

## Dynamic warm-up

1. **Frontal stance drill** — 4 rounds of 2 minutes. Do slip – throw 2 punches, with steps forward/backward.
2. **Fighting stance drill** — do slip – slip – throw 2 punches, with steps forward/backward.

*20 knuckle push-ups and 20 mountain climbers.*

3. **Movement drills with a tire** — 2 rounds of 2 minutes. Go around in both directions, in orthodox stance and in southpaw stance.

*12 jump squats.*

4. **Shadow boxing** — 1 round of 2 minutes. Throw a punch and dodge/block the same one.

*10 burpees.*

## Bag work — 6 rounds of 3 minutes

1. Movement drill with a bag
2. Approach — any defensive move — throw any single punch or a combo — exit
3. Approach — jab — defence — throw any 2 punches — exit
4. Defensive drill
5. Throw a punch and then dodge the same one
6. Conditioning drill (4 minutes)

## Conditioning drill

Medicine ball throws (straight punches, to the sides, down and up) — 6–8 reps for each exercise.

## Finisher

- Knuckle push-ups, squats, balanced tucks — 30 seconds each without rest, then 1 minute of rest.
- Plyometric push-ups (8–12), jump squats (8–12), hanging leg raises (max).

Finish with stretches.
