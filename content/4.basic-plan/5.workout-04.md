# Workout 4

## Dynamic warm-up

1. **Frontal stance drill** — 1 round of 3 minutes: 1 — move forward and backward, 2 — add single punches, 3 — throw combos.
2. **Fighting stance drill** — 1 round of 3 minutes: 1 — move forward and backward, 2 — add single punches, 3 — throw combos.
3. **Defensive drill** — 1 round of 3 minutes: 1 — head movement, 2 — add footwork, 3 — hands up.
4. **Shadow boxing drills with the ladder** — 6 rounds of 1 minute: 1 — jab with every step, 2 — cross with every step, 3 — jab–cross, 4, 5, 6 — same but to the sides.

## Virtual sparring against a slugger — 5 rounds of 3 minutes

- Only lead hand
- Only 1 punch of the rear hand per attack
- Freestyle
- Change stance

## Finisher

- Drills with resistance bands — 1 round of 3 minutes.
- Agility footwork drill — 1 round of 3 minutes.

Finish with static stretches.
