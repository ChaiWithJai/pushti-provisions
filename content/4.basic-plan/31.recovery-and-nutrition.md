# Recovery and nutrition

## Recovery tips

- **Hydration** — staying well-hydrated aids in nutrient transport and toxin removal, crucial for efficient recovery.
- **Stretching** — stretching prevents muscle tightness and promotes flexibility, reducing the risk of injury.
- **Active rest** — low intensity body movements such as walking can reduce lactic acid build-up and pump necessary fluids to sore muscles for efficient recovery.
- **Nutrition** — eating additional protein will help you recover at a much faster rate. Try to aim for 1 g per pound of lean body weight — use the [lean body weight calculator](https://www.calculator.net/lean-body-mass-calculator.html).
- **Mental relaxation** — mental relaxation techniques reduce stress, ensuring a positive mindset for optimal recovery and performance.

## Nutrition and the meal plan

## Rest days

Days **7, 14, 21, 28 and 35** are full rest days. "Don't forget these!" — even on rest days, keep the [daily to-do](/basic-plan#daily-to-do) (sleep, positive attitude, fasting, cardio, meals, water) and the daily stretch routine.
