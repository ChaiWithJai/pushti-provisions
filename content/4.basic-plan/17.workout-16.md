# Workout 16

::callout{kind="info" title="Numbering note"}
The PDF labels this page "Boxing Workout #15" a second time (and later skips #16) — by sequence, this is the plan's 16th boxing workout.
::

## Dynamic warm-up

1. **Frontal stance drill** — 4 rounds of 2 minutes: 1 — replace feet, 2 — replace feet and rotate shoulders, 3 — load your hips, 4 — add a single punch.
2. **Fighting stance drill** — 3 rounds of 2 minutes: 1 — slips with steps forward, 2 — slips and shifts, 3 — slips, shift and a punch.

*20 knuckle push-ups and 20 mountain climbers.*

3. **Movement drills with a tire** — 2 rounds of 2 minutes. Go around in both directions and throw punches, in orthodox stance and in southpaw stance, with pivots and shifts.

*12 jump squats.*

4. **Shadow boxing** — 1 round of 2 minutes. Move in all directions and throw the 1-2-3 combo, then dodge the same one.

*10 burpees.*

## Bag work — 6 rounds of 3 minutes

1. Movement drill with a bag
2. Approach — slip, weight shift and a 2-punch combo — exit
3. Approach — any straight punch — slip + weight shift — throw any 2 punches — exit
4. Defensive drill
5. Make the bag swing and throw only simultaneous counters
6. Conditioning drill (2 rounds of 75 seconds)

## Conditioning drill

Medicine ball throws (straight punches, to the sides, down and up) — 6–8 reps for each exercise. Throw the ball off the wall.

## Finisher

- Knuckle push-ups, squats, balanced tucks — 30 seconds each without rest, then 1 minute of rest.
- Plyometric push-ups (8–12), jump squats (8–12), hanging leg raises (max).

Finish with stretches.
