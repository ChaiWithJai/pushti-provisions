# Basic Plan

The Basic Plan ("Total Boxing Beginner", by Andrii Khotin & Coach Isaac) is the beginner training plan of the KO Boxing Package. It is built around **8 workouts per week — 5 boxing workouts and 3 conditioning (weightlifting) workouts** — which you should adjust to fit your own schedule.

The plan runs for five weeks. Each week you work through five numbered boxing workouts and three numbered lifting workouts, and every seventh day (days 7, 14, 21, 28 and 35) is a full rest day. After day 35 the instruction is simple: **restart the program with better technique!**

- **Boxing workouts** mix dynamic warm-ups (stance, movement and defensive drills), shadow boxing (including sets of 12 punch combos that evolve week by week), bag work, virtual pad work and virtual sparring rounds, plus conditioning finishers (medicine ball throws, tennis ball drills, resistance band rounds, bodyweight circuits).
- **Lifting workouts** are collected on the [Weightlifting workouts](/basic-plan/weightlifting) page. On some lift days the PDF offers a "Don't feel like lifting today?!" alternative running workout.
- **Recovery guidance and nutrition** — hydration, stretching, active rest, protein targets and the meal plan — are on the [Recovery and nutrition](/basic-plan/recovery-and-nutrition) page.
- **Mindset and lifestyle tips** from the PDF (productivity, gratitude, benefits of boxing) are on the [Mindset and lifestyle](/basic-plan/mindset-and-lifestyle) page.

## Daily to-do

The PDF repeats this checklist on almost every page — it applies **every day**, training or not:

- 7–8 hours of sleep
- Positive attitude
- 14 hour fast (water only)
- Cardio workout
- 3 high protein meals — see the [meal plan](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1mLJtuKeCIFc4wX_DfA4FH6SmoarMV8-Hp9IQWDqPjLE/edit?usp=sharing)
- 1 gallon of water

## Daily stretch

::callout{kind="warning" title="Super important!"}
Do the daily stretch routine every day:

- Forward fold
- Trunk twist
- Assisted lunge
- Child's pose
::

Each workout page below keeps its own drills in full and simply refers back here for the daily to-do and daily stretch.
