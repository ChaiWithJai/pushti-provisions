# Workout 21

## Dynamic warm-up

1. **Frontal stance drills** — 4 rounds of 2 minutes: 1 — side lunges, 2 — side lunges with feet rotation, 3 — move the other foot, 4 — add a single punch.
2. **Fighting stance drill** — 4 rounds of 2 minutes: 1 — side lunges, 2 — side lunges with feet rotation, 3 — move the other foot, 4 — add a single punch.

*20 knuckle push-ups and 20 mountain climbers.*

3. **Movement drills with a tire** — 2 rounds of 2 minutes. Go around in both directions and throw punches, in orthodox stance and in southpaw stance, with pivots, shifts and sidesteps.

*12 jump squats.*

4. **Shadow boxing** — 1 round of 2 minutes. Move in all directions and throw the 1-1-2 combo, then dodge the same one.

*10 burpees.*

## Bag work — 6 rounds of 3 minutes

1. Movement drill with a bag
2. Approach — slip, pivot or weight shift and a 2-punch combo — exit
3. Approach — any straight punch — slip + pivot or weight shift — throw any 2 punches — exit
4. Defensive drill
5. Make the bag swing and throw only sidestep counters
6. Pick your favorite 2-punch combo and throw it as fast as possible 30 times. Rest for 30 seconds, then throw it as hard as possible 15 times

## Conditioning drill

Medicine ball throws (straight punches, to the sides, down and up) — 6–8 reps for each exercise. Throw the ball off the wall.

## Finisher

- Knuckle push-ups, squats, balanced tucks — 30 seconds each without rest, then 1 minute of rest.
- Plyometric push-ups (8–12), jump squats (8–12), hanging leg raises (max).

Finish with stretches.
