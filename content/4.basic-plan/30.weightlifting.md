# Weightlifting workouts

The Basic Plan schedules **three lifting workouts per week**, numbered Lift 1 through Lift 15 across the five weeks (Lifts 1–3 in week 1, 4–6 in week 2, 7–9 in week 3, 10–12 in week 4, 13–15 in week 5). Several lift numbers repeat the same session — identical sessions are grouped below.

Every session starts with a **warm-up** and ends with the **stretch series**. In the PDF each session is laid out as four numbered exercise blocks (#1–#4) with photo demonstrations: the first rep scheme applies to blocks 1–2, the second to blocks 3–4. Exercises are listed in the order they appear.

## Lifts 1, 5, 10 and 11

Warm up, then:

- Blocks 1–2: **4 sets of 5 reps** each exercise
- Blocks 3–4: **2 sets of 8–10 reps** each exercise

Exercises: Hex Bar Deadlift · High Plank Y Raise · Squat Rocks · Split Squats · Dumbbell Z Press · Reverse Y Raise · Bench Press · Pendlay Rows · Pull-Ups

Finish with the Stretch Series.

## Lifts 2, 7 and 13

Warm up, then:

- Blocks 1–2: **3 sets of 10 reps** each exercise
- Blocks 3–4: **2 sets of 5–8 reps** each exercise

Exercises: Kettlebell Swings · Med Ball Slams · Squat Rocks · Split Jumps · Broad to 180° Jump · Vertical Jump · Landmine Press · Switch Press · Squat Jumps

Finish with the Stretch Series.

## Lift 3

Warm up, then:

- Blocks 1–2: **3 sets of 10 reps** each exercise
- Blocks 3–4: **2 sets of 5–8 reps** each exercise

Exercises: Squat · Hip Airplanes · Depth Drop · Plyo Push-Up · Inverted Row · Dumbbell Overhead Walk (20 steps each) · Pallof Press · Scap Push-Up · Kettlebell Swing

Finish with the Stretch Series.

## Lifts 4, 8 and 14

Warm up, then:

- Blocks 1–2: **3 sets of 10 reps** each exercise
- Blocks 3–4: **2 sets of 5–8 reps** each exercise — for **Lift 8**, the PDF specifies **3 sets of 5–8 reps** instead

Exercises: Kettlebell Swings · Dumbbell Press · Copenhagen Planks · Skater Reactions · Plate Hops · Squat Jumps · Seated Z Press · Pull-Ups · Dumbbell Snatch

Finish with the Stretch Series.

## Lifts 6 and 9

Warm up, then:

- Blocks 1–2: **3 sets of 8 reps** each exercise
- Blocks 3–4: **3 sets of 10 reps** each exercise

Exercises: Front Squat · Long Hip Bridge · Seated Vertical · Hanging Leg Raise · Copenhagen Plank · High Plank Bird Dogs · Pallof Press · Scap Push-Up · Box Jump

Finish with the Stretch Series.

## Lifts 12 and 15

Warm up, then:

- Blocks 1–2: **3 sets of 8 reps** each exercise
- Blocks 3–4: **3 sets of 10 reps** each exercise

Exercises: Back Squat · Glute Bridge Opens · Continuous Broads · Hanging Leg Raise · Copenhagen Plank · High Plank Shoulder Taps · Med Ball Wall Toss · Med Ball Chest Pass · Skater Hops

Finish with the Stretch Series.

## Don't feel like lifting today?!

On the pages for Lifts 5, 8 and 14 the PDF offers an **alternative running workout** instead of the lift. The running workout itself is a linked video demonstration — you'll find it on the [Video demonstrations](/basic-plan/videos) page.
