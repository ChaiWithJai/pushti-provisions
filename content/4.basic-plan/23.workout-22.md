# Workout 22

## Dynamic warm-up

1. **Frontal stance drill** — 3 rounds of 2 minutes: 1 — move back and do sidesteps, 2 — throw single punches with the side step, 3 — 2 punches + sidestep punch.
2. **Fighting stance drill** — 3 rounds of 2 minutes: 1 — move back and do sidesteps, 2 — throw single punches with the side step, 3 — 2 punches + sidestep punch.
3. **Frontal stance defensive drill** — 1 round of 3 minutes. Move in a circle, throw punches, and do slip – slip – roll – roll.
4. **Footwork drill** — throw uppercuts for 2 rounds of 1 minute, with 30 seconds of rest in between.

## Virtual pad work — 5 rounds of 3 minutes

1. All single punches with steps back, in frontal stance
2. Sidestep counters, in frontal stance
3. Punches with steps back, in the fighting stance
4. Any defence + 2 punches
5. Jab + any defence + 2 punches

## Finisher

- **Fighting stance** — 1 round of 3 minutes. Move with your hands down and practice defensive moves.
- Agility footwork — 2 rounds.

Finish with stretches.
