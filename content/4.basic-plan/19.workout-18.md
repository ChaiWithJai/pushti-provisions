# Workout 18

## Shadow boxing — practice 5 times each of the 12 combos

1. Jab – Cross body
2. Jab – Slip inside – Rear hook
3. Jab – Cross – Lead hook – Lead body uppercut
4. Jab – Slip outside – Lead uppercut – Rear hook
5. Jab – Slip + weight shift – Rear hook body – Lead hook
6. Jab – Cross – Slip + weight shift – Lead hook body – Rear hook
7. Lead hook body – Rear hook body – Slip + shift – Lead hook
8. Rear hook body – Lead hook body – Slip + shift – Rear hook
9. Lead hook body – Lead uppercut – Slip + shift – Rear hook
10. Rear hook body – Rear uppercut – Slip + shift – Lead hook
11. Lead hook – Rear hook body – Lead hook body – Rear hook
12. Rear hook – Lead hook body – Rear hook body – Lead hook

*10 plyometric push-ups and 30 mountain climbers right after shadow boxing.*

## Defensive drill

1 round of 3 minutes: 1 — head movement, 2 — add footwork, 3 — hands up.

## Bag work — 7 rounds of 3 minutes, 1 minute of rest in between

| Round | Work |
|-------|------|
| 1 | Combos 1 and 2 |
| 2 | Combos 3 and 4 |
| 3 | Combos 5 and 6 |
| 4 | Combos 7 and 8 |
| 5 | Combos 9 and 10 |
| 6 | Combos 11 and 12 |
| 7 | Voice-controlled boxing: "I say a combo — you throw it" (round 4 of the linked video) |

## Finisher

- Speed and accuracy drill with a tennis ball — 1 round of 3 minutes.
- Shadow boxing with dumbbells.
- 1 set of max reps: parallel bar dips, pull-ups, jumping squat lunges and hanging leg raises. Rest for 1 minute between exercises.

Finish with stretches.
