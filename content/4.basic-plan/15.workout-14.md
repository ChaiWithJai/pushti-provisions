# Workout 14

## Dynamic warm-up

1. **Frontal stance drills** — 4 rounds of 1 minute. Touch the floor with a step back and throw 2 punches, with steps forward or backward.
2. **Fighting stance drill** — practice each of the 6 basic punches (jab, cross, hooks, uppercuts) for 45 seconds, with 15 seconds of rest in between.
3. **Defensive drill** — jab – cross – roll – roll, and slip – slip – hook – hook.
4. **Fighting stance ladder drills** — 3 rounds of 2 minutes. Move and throw punch combos: 1 — forward/backward, 2 — to the sides, 3 — pivots.

## Virtual sparring against a southpaw — 5 rounds of 3 minutes

- Only lead hand
- 1 rear hand punch per attack
- Freestyle
- Change stance

## Finisher

- Drills with resistance bands — 1 round of 3 minutes.
- Agility footwork drill — 2 rounds.

Finish with stretches.
