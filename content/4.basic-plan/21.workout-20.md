# Workout 20

## Dynamic warm-up

1. 1 round of 3 minutes — punch with a step forward, forward and to the side, and backward.

*10 jump squats.*

## Shadow boxing — 5 rounds of 3 minutes

1. Only movement
2. Only long range attacks
3. Only close range attacks
4. Freestyle
5. Change the stance

## Virtual angle creation and counterattacking

1 round of 3 minutes. "I throw single punches, you block and dodge them." (Round 4 of the linked video.)

*20 skater hops.*

## Bag work — 6 rounds of 3 minutes

1. Only movement
2. Only long range attacks
3. Only close range attacks
4. Freestyle
5. Change the stance
6. Throw each of the 6 basic punches with full strength, 10 times each

## Finisher

- Speed and accuracy with a dumbbell — 1 round of 2 minutes.
- Shadow boxing with resistance bands — 6 rounds of 1 minute with 20 seconds of rest.
- 1 set of 80–90% of max reps, with 1 minute of rest between the exercises:
  - Knuckle push-ups
  - Squats with a med ball throw
  - Pull-ups
  - Sit-ups with med ball throws

Finish with stretches.

> If it were truly easy, then everyone would be doing it... Do you want to be like everyone else?
