# Workout 7

## Dynamic warm-up

1. **Frontal stance drill** — 4 rounds of 2 minutes. Do roll – roll – throw 2 punches, with steps forward/backward.
2. **Fighting stance drill** — 4 rounds of 2 minutes. Do roll – roll – throw 2 punches, with steps forward/backward.
3. **Fighting stance drill** — 1 round of 2 minutes. 2 jumps up, and on the 3rd one throw any punch with a step in any direction.
4. **Frontal stance** — 4 rounds of 2 minutes. Move at one spot and throw 2 punches with steps forward/backward/to the sides.
5. **Fighting stance** — 1 round of 2 minutes. Move at one spot and throw 2 punches with steps forward/backward/to the sides.

## Virtual pad work — 5 rounds of 3 minutes

1. Single simultaneous counters in frontal stance
2. Defensive move + 2 punches in frontal stance
3. Single simultaneous counters in fighting stance
4. Defensive move + 2 punches in fighting stance
5. Single punches in fighting stance with steps back

## Finisher

- **Frontal stance** — 1 round of 3 minutes. Move with your hands down and practice defensive moves.
- Agility footwork — 2 rounds.

Finish with stretches.
