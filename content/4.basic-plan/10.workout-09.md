# Workout 9

## Dynamic warm-up

1. **Frontal stance drill** — 2 rounds of 1.5 minutes: 1 — move forward and throw any punches, 2 — move backwards and throw any punches.
2. **Fighting stance drill** — 1 round of 3 minutes: 1 — move forward and backward, 2 — add single punches, 3 — throw combos.
3. **Defensive drill** — 1 round of 3 minutes: 1 — head movement, 2 — add footwork, 3 — hands up.
4. **Fighting stance ladder drills** — 3 rounds of 2 minutes. Move and throw single punches: 1 — forward/backward, 2 — to the sides, 3 — Z steps, 4 — L steps.

## Virtual sparring against a puncher — 5 rounds of 3 minutes

- Only lead hand
- Only 1 punch of the rear hand per attack
- Freestyle
- Change stance

## Finisher

- Drills with resistance bands — 1 round of 3 minutes.
- Agility footwork drill — 2 rounds.

Finish with stretches.
