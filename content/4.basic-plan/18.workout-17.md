# Workout 17

## Dynamic warm-up

1. **Frontal stance drill** — 3 rounds of 2 minutes: 1 — move and do shifts, 2 — shift + throw 2 punches, 3 — 2 punches + shift + 2 punches forward.
2. **Fighting stance drill** — 3 rounds of 2 minutes: 1 — move and do shifts, 2 — shift + throw 2 punches, 3 — 2 punches + shift + 2 punches forward.
3. **Fighting stance drill** — 1 round of 2 minutes. 2 jumps up and then 2 punches in any direction, and repeat.
4. **Frontal stance drill** — straights, hooks and uppercuts in both stances. 6 rounds of 45 seconds, with 15 seconds of rest in between.

*20 push-ups and 20 mountain climbers.*

## Virtual pad work — 5 rounds of 3 minutes

1. Punch – slip outside – punch – slip inside, in frontal stance
2. Slip + shift + 1 punch, in frontal stance
3. Punch – slip outside – punch – slip inside, in frontal stance
4. Slip + shift + 1 or 2 punches, in the fighting stance
5. Jab + any defence + 2 punches

## Finisher

- **Fighting stance** — 1 round of 3 minutes. Move with your hands down and practice defensive moves in the opposite stance.
- Agility footwork — 2 rounds.

Finish with stretches.
