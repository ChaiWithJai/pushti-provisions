# Workout 12

::callout{kind="info" title="Numbering note"}
The PDF labels this page "Boxing Workout #11" a second time (and later skips #12) — by sequence, this is the plan's 12th boxing workout.
::

## Dynamic warm-up

1. **Frontal stance drill** — 4 rounds of 2 minutes: 1 — move and do pivots, 2 — throw 2 punches + pivot, 3 — 2 punches + pivot + 2 punches forward, 4 — 2 punches + pivot + 2 punches backward.
2. **Fighting stance drill** — 4 rounds of 2 minutes: 1 — move and do pivots, 2 — throw 2 punches + pivot, 3 — 2 punches + pivot + 2 punches forward, 4 — 2 punches + pivot + 2 punches backward.
3. **Fighting stance drill** — 1 round of 2 minutes. 2 jumps up and then 2 punches in any direction, and repeat.
4. **Frontal stance** — 1 round of 2 minutes. Straight punch, slip inside, any punch.
5. **Fighting stance** — 1 round of 2 minutes. Straight punch, slip inside, any punch.

## Virtual pad work — 5 rounds of 3 minutes

1. Slip + pivot + 1 punch
2. Defensive move + pivot + 1 punch in fighting stance
3. Defensive move + pivot + 2 punches in fighting stance
4. Jab + any defence + 2 punches
5. Jab + any defence + pivot + 2 punches

## Finisher

- **Fighting stance** — 1 round of 3 minutes. Move with your hands down and practice defensive moves.
- Agility footwork — 2 rounds.

Finish with stretches.
