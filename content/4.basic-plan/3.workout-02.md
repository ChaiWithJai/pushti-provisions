# Workout 2

## Dynamic warm-up

1. **Frontal stance** — 4 rounds of 2 minutes. Move at one spot and throw punches with a step forward/backward/to the sides.
2. **Fighting stance** — 4 rounds of 2 minutes. Move at one spot and throw punches with a step forward/backward/to the sides.
3. **Frontal stance** — 1 round of 2 minutes. Move in a circle and throw any punches.
4. **Fighting stance movement ladder drill** — 3 rounds of 2 minutes: 1 — forward/backward, 2 — to the sides, 3 — Z and L steps.
5. **Frontal stance defensive drill** — 1 round of 2 minutes. Move at one spot and touch your knee + punch.
6. **Fighting stance defensive drill** — 1 round of 2 minutes. Move at one spot and touch your knee + punch.

## Virtual pad work — 5 rounds of 3 minutes

1. All single punches in frontal stance with rotation or step
2. Defensive move + single punches in frontal stance
3. All single punches in fighting stance with rotation or step
4. 2-punch combos in fighting stance
5. Defensive move + single punches in fighting stance

## Finisher

- Throw punches with every step with dumbbells — 1 round of 2 minutes.
- Agility footwork — 2 rounds.

Finish with stretches.
