# Boxing lessons

The core curriculum of the KO Boxing Package: twenty video lessons on Andrii's YouTube channel, embedded below in the order the package presents them. Work through Part 1 before starting a plan — the workouts assume you know these techniques by name.

::callout{kind="tip" title="How to study"}
Take your time to absorb these lessons and practice your form, especially if you're new to boxing. Dedicating a few days or even weeks to the fundamentals will make the program far easier to follow.
::
