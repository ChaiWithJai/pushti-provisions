# Week 5

::callout{kind="info" title="Main focus: Peak performance phase (fight readiness)"}
Achieve peak physical conditioning, sharpen fight skills, and build the mental edge needed for fight night.

Key objectives:

- **Maximize conditioning:** fine-tune speed, power, and endurance while tapering training to stay fresh.
- **Precision and timing:** focus on sharp, efficient techniques and fight-specific drills.
- **Fight mentality:** develop confidence, composure, and tactical awareness to execute under pressure.

This phase ensures you're physically primed, mentally locked in, and ready to perform at your best in the ring.
::

## Boxing workout 21

### Dynamic warm-up

- 5 burpees; 20 jumping jacks

### Shadow boxing

Go for 3 rounds of 3 minutes, with 1 minute of rest in between, of limited freestyle shadow boxing:

1. Use only the lead hand — 1 round of 3 minutes
2. Use only the rear hand — 1 round of 3 minutes
3. Freestyle work with focus on combos — 1 round of 3 minutes

### Bag work

3 rounds of 3 minutes, with 1 minute of rest in between, of freestyle bag work imitating a real fight:

1. Learning the opponent and setting traps
2. Establishing dominance and utilising the traps
3. Giving everything you have got

### Finisher

- 1 set of 50% of max reps: push-ups, squats, balanced tucks, pull-ups
- 1 set of 6–8 reps: clap push-up, squat jumps, hanging leg raises

### Cool-down

Finish the session with the cool-down routine (linked in the PDF).

::callout{kind="tip" title="Coach's note"}
Precision beats power, timing beats speed.
::

## Boxing workout 23

### Dynamic warm-up

- Frontal stance: move at one spot and throw punches with a step forward/backward/to the sides. 4 rounds of 2 minutes
- Fighting stance: move at one spot and throw punches with a step forward/backward/to the sides. 4 rounds of 2 minutes
- 20 skater hops; 10 shoulder touches; 10 run-outs

### Shadow boxing

| Drill | Work | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fighting stance: slip rope counters | 1 round × 3 min | — |
| Fighting stance: attack and defend the same attack | 1 round × 3 min | — |
| Fighting stance: freestyle work with 3 second sprints | 1 round × 3 min | — |

### Bag work — 9 rounds of 1 minute, focus on speed

Pick your 3 favourite combos and practice each of them for a whole 1-minute round, so you will practice each combo for 3 rounds. Focus on being realistic, as if you were in a fight, and go for speed rather than power.

### Finisher

Light freestyle shadow boxing (3 rounds of 1 minute):

1. With bands
2. With dumbbells
3. Without anything

### Cool-down

Finish the session with the cool-down routine (linked in the PDF).

::callout{kind="tip" title="Coach's note"}
Everyone has a plan until they get punched in the face.

## Boxing workout 25

### Dynamic warm-up

- 10 frog jumps with punches; 20 side-to-side jumps; 10 pogo jumps

### Visualization shadow boxing

Imagine a specific person in front of you and fight them. 3 rounds of 3 minutes with 1 minute of rest in between.

### Virtual sparring — 3 rounds of 3 minutes

1. Freestyle
2. Freestyle
3. Freestyle

### Extra drills (page sidebar)

The PDF prints this additional block of drills beside the workout:

- 6 rounds of 3 minutes of bag work:
  1. Only movement
  2. Only long range attacks
  3. Only close range attacks
  4. Freestyle
  5. Change the stance
  6. Conditioning bag work drill (1 round)
- Tennis ball to the wall drill. 1 round of 3 minutes

### Finisher

- Agility footwork drills: 5 rounds of 45 seconds with 15 seconds of rest in between

### Cool-down

Finish the session with the cool-down routine and stretches.

## Active recovery workout 5

The final active recovery page carries no drill list — it closes the plan with a reminder of what the sport gives back:

::callout{kind="info" title="Benefits of boxing"}
- **Health:** boxing enhances cardiovascular fitness and endurance through intense workouts, improving heart health.
- **Full-body strength:** this sport engages various muscle groups, leading to increased strength and muscle tone.
- **Stress relief:** boxing provides an outlet for stress and releases endorphins, promoting mental well-being.
- **Self-confidence:** as boxing skills progress, so does self-assurance, boosting confidence inside and outside the ring.
- **Self-defense skills:** boxing equips you with practical self-defense techniques, enhancing personal safety and confidence in challenging situations.
::

*Champions aren't made in gyms. Champions are made from something they have deep inside them — a desire, a dream, a vision.*
