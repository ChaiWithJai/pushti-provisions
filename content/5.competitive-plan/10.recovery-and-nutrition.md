# Recovery and nutrition

Nearly every page of the PDF repeats two sidebars — a daily to-do checklist and a daily stretch routine — flagged as "Super important!". They apply every day of the plan, including rest days.

## Daily to-do

- 7–8 hours of sleep
- Positive attitude
- 14-hour fast (water only)
- Cardio workout
- 3 high-protein meals
- 1 gallon of water

## Daily stretch

- Forward fold
- Trunk twist
- Assisted lunge
- Child's pose

## Recovery tips

- **Hydration:** staying well-hydrated aids in nutrient transport and toxin removal, crucial for efficient recovery.
- **Stretching:** stretching prevents muscle tightness and promotes flexibility, reducing the risk of injury.
- **Active rest:** low-intensity body movements such as walking can reduce lactic acid build-up and pump necessary fluids to sore muscles for efficient recovery.
- **Nutrition:** eating additional protein will help you recover at a much faster rate. Try to aim for 1 g per pound of lean body weight — use the [lean body mass calculator](https://www.calculator.net/lean-body-mass-calculator.html) to find yours.
- **Mental relaxation:** mental relaxation techniques reduce stress, ensuring a positive mindset for optimal recovery and performance.
