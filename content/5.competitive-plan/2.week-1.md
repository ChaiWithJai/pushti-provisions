# Week 1

::callout{kind="info" title="Main focus: General preparation phase (foundation building)"}
This phase is all about laying a strong foundation for your boxing performance. The goal is to build overall physical fitness, reinforce fundamental techniques, and develop the mental discipline required for high-level training.

Key objectives:

- **Conditioning:** improve endurance, strength, and mobility to prepare your body for more intense training in later phases.
- **Fundamentals:** sharpen basic techniques like footwork, head movement, and punch mechanics to ensure efficiency and control.
- **Discipline and consistency:** establish a solid training routine and mindset to maintain focus and long-term progress.

This phase is essential for injury prevention, skill refinement, and overall performance development before moving into more specialized training.
::

## Boxing workout 2

### Dynamic warm-up

- Frontal stance: move at one spot and throw punches with a step forward/backward/to the sides. 4 rounds of 2 minutes
- Fighting stance: move at one spot and throw punches with a step forward/backward/to the sides. 4 rounds of 2 minutes
- 10 clap push-ups; 10 knees-to-chest jumps; 20 climbers

### Shadow boxing

| Drill | Work | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frontal stance: leg swing and punch | 1 round × 2 min (1 min each side) | — |
| Frontal stance: move forward & backward and attack | 2 rounds × 2 min | 30 s between rounds |
| Frontal stance: 90° power hooks | 1 round × 2 min | — |
| Fighting stance: move forward and backward with attacks | 2 rounds × 2 min | 30 s between rounds |
| Postman's blow combo | 1 round × 2 min | — |
| Bounce up and throw jab-cross | 1 round × 3 min | — |

### Partner work — 5 rounds of 3 minutes

Switch roles after each attack:

1. N1: any straight punch. N2: any hand defence
2. N1: any straight punch – slip – any punch. N2: block – any straight punch – block
3. N1: jab – slip – lead uppercut – cross. N2: catch – jab – block – any straight punch
4. N1: jab – step back – lead hook – cross. N2: catch – jab – block – any straight punch
5. N1: any single punch. N2: any counter attack

### Bag work — 2 rounds of 3 minutes

- Freestyle work

### Finisher

- 1 set of: push-ups, squats, hanging leg raises, superman raises, pull-ups, neck (yes, no, maybe)

### Cool-down

Finish the session with the cool-down routine (linked in the PDF).

## Boxing workout 3

### Dynamic warm-up

- Frontal stance: move at one spot and throw punches with a step forward/backward/to the sides. 4 rounds of 2 minutes
- Fighting stance: move at one spot and throw punches with a step forward/backward/to the sides. 4 rounds of 2 minutes
- 20 skater hops; 10 shoulder touches; 10 run-outs

### Shadow boxing

| Drill | Work | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frontal stance: knee touch + punch | 1 round × 2 min | — |
| Frontal stance: slip – punch – slip – punch – move | 1 round × 2 min | — |
| Frontal stance: slip – slip – roll – roll | 1 round × 2 min | — |
| Fighting stance: jab – any defence – any 2 punches | 1 round × 2 min | — |
| Fighting stance: slip – 3 punches with steps | 1 round × 2 min | — |
| Fighting stance: pendulum step — punch forward-backward | 1 round × 2 min | — |

### Bag work — 10 rounds of 2 minutes

One combo per round:

1. Jab – cross
2. Jab – cross – step back – cross
3. Jab – step back – lead hook – cross
4. Jab – step back – cross – lead hook – cross
5. Jab – jab – cross body
6. Jab – slip – lead hook body – rear hook – lead hook
7. Jab – cross – slip – rear hook body – lead hook
8. Cross – lead hook – lead hook body
9. Jab – jab – cross – slip – rear hook body
10. Freestyle work using combos that you practiced

### Agility ladder drills — 10 reps each drill

- Run forward
- Icky shuffle
- Icky shuffle back
- In & out jumps
- In & out jumps backwards
- Both feet in & out forward
- Both feet in & out backward
- Lateral in & out jumps with core twists
