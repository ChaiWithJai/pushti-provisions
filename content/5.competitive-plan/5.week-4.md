# Week 4

::callout{kind="info" title="Main focus: Specialized preparation phase (skill & conditioning optimization)"}
This phase is dedicated to refining specific boxing skills, strategies, and physical attributes essential for high-level performance in the ring.

Key objectives:

- **Skill development:** focus on advanced techniques, ring IQ, and strategic execution tailored to your fighting style.
- **Conditioning optimization:** enhance fight-specific endurance, strength, and explosiveness to maintain peak performance throughout a bout.
- **Tactical application:** implement fight scenarios, controlled sparring, and opponent-specific preparation to sharpen adaptability and decision-making.

This phase bridges the gap between general training and fight readiness, ensuring you're physically and mentally prepared for competition.
::

| Fighting stance: move backward and counter | 1 round × 2 min | — |
| Fighting stance: pendulum step combo | 1 round × 2 min | — |
| Fighting stance: pendulum step combo | 1 round × 2 min | — |

### Partner work — 6 rounds of 4 minutes

Switch roles after each attack:

1. N1: attacks with any 2 punch combo. N2: defends combos
2. N1: jab – cross – step back – cross – hook. N2: catch – jab – block
3. N1: jab – any defence – any single counter. N2: reply to the provocation with a straight punch – block
4. N1: jab – any defence – any combo counter. N2: reply to the provocation with a straight punch – block
5. N1: at the close range, any single punch. N2: block and reply with the same hand 2 punch combo
6. Sparring

### Bag work — 1 round of 4 minutes

- Freestyle work

### Finisher

- 1 set of: push-ups, squats, hanging leg raises, superman raises, pull-ups, neck (yes, no, maybe)

## Boxing workout 18

### Dynamic warm-up

- Frontal stance: move at one spot and throw punches with a step forward/backward/to the sides. 4 rounds of 2 minutes
- Fighting stance: move at one spot and throw punches with a step forward/backward/to the sides. 4 rounds of 2 minutes
- 20 skater hops; 10 shoulder touches; 10 run-outs

### Shadow boxing

| Drill | Work | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fighting stance: create angles | 1 round × 3 min | — |
| Fighting stance: avoiding ropes | 1 round × 3 min | — |
| Fighting stance: defence – create an angle – attack | 1 round × 3 min | — |
| Fighting stance: foot positioning drill | 1 round × 3 min | — |
| Fighting stance: defend the postman's blow | 1 round × 2 min | — |

### Bag work — 10 rounds of 2 minutes

Add 10 push-ups and 10 squats during rest periods. Each round pairs a set-up with its follow-up:

1. Jab body — fake jab – overhand
2. Jab — long lead uppercut – cross
3. Jab-cross — jab – feint cross – hook
4. Jab-jab-cross — spinning jab – jab – rear hook
5. Jab — foot and hand feint – jab-cross
6. Jab — stop with the high guard — pull back counter
7. Hook body – hook body – hook head — hook body – hook body – uppercut head
8. Jab – slip – long lead uppercut — jab – step-in slip – liver hook – overhand
9. Jab – cross – liver hook — jab – cross – narrow solar plexus uppercut
10. Freestyle using the set-ups

### Finisher

- Get up from a chair and throw a power punch: 20 reps for each of the 8 basic punches
- 12 reps for each exercise: box jumps, depth drop, step-up jump, Bulgarian split squat jump

### Cool-down

Finish the session with the cool-down routine (linked in the PDF).

3. Rolls
4. Plate punches
5. Side jumps
6. Plank
7. Landmine punches
8. Boxing steps with weights
9. Wall sit
10. Barbell push-outs

Finish with the stretch series.
