# Week 2

::callout{kind="info" title="Main focus: Specialized preparation phase (skill & conditioning optimization)"}
This phase is dedicated to refining specific boxing skills, strategies, and physical attributes essential for high-level performance in the ring.

Key objectives:

- **Skill development:** focus on advanced techniques, ring IQ, and strategic execution tailored to your fighting style.
- **Conditioning optimization:** enhance fight-specific endurance, strength, and explosiveness to maintain peak performance throughout a bout.
- **Tactical application:** implement fight scenarios, controlled sparring, and opponent-specific preparation to sharpen adaptability and decision-making.

This phase bridges the gap between general training and fight readiness, ensuring you're physically and mentally prepared for competition.
::

## Boxing workout 6

### Dynamic warm-up

- 10 push-ups; 6 360° jumps; 10 jump half squats with punches

### Shadow boxing

| Drill | Work | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frontal stance: pivoting drill | 3 rounds × 2 min | 30 s between rounds |
| Frontal stance: shuffle step drill | 1 round × 3 min | — |
| Fighting stance: attack – defence – attack | 3 rounds × 1 min | 20 s between rounds |
| Fighting stance: pendulum step in a circle | 1 round × 2 min | — |
| Fighting stance: pivoting drill | 1 round × 2 min | — |

### Bag work — 6 rounds of 3 minutes

One focus per round:

1. Only lead hand
2. Only rear hand
3. Only pendulum step attacks
4. Close range
5. Freestyle with focus on 3–4 punch combos
6. 3 second sprints with freestyle work

### Drills with a weighted stick — each drill for 1 minute

- Core rotations with hooks (each side)
- Jumping push-outs
- Slip uppercut (each side)
- Wrist rotation (each side)
- Squat and lift the stick
- Switched step punches (each side)

### Cool-down

Finish the session with the cool-down routine (linked in the PDF).

## Boxing workout 7

### Dynamic warm-up

- Frontal stance: move at one spot and throw punches with a step forward/backward/to the sides. 4 rounds of 2 minutes
- Fighting stance: move at one spot and throw punches with a step forward/backward/to the sides. 4 rounds of 2 minutes
- 10 clap push-ups; 10 knees-to-chest jumps; 20 climbers

### Shadow boxing

| Drill | Work | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frontal stance: squat and attack | 4 rounds × 1 min | 20 s between rounds |
| Frontal stance: bounce and punch | 1 round × 2 min | — |
| Fighting stance: attack – pivot – attack | 1 round × 2 min | — |
| Fighting stance: step and hit | 1 round × 2 min | — |
| Fighting stance: attack – defence | 1 round × 2 min | — |
| Fighting stance: pendulum step drill | 1 round × 2 min | — |

### Partner work — 6 rounds of 4 minutes

Switch roles after each attack:

1. N1: any single punch. N2: any defensive move
2. N1: jab – step back – any 2 punches. N2: catch – straight punch – block
3. N1: jab – slip – pivot – lead hook – rear hook body. N2: catch – straight punch – shield block – block
4. N1: any straight punch – slip – hook body – hook head. N2: catch – elbow block – shield block
5. Light sparring with single punches
6. Freestyle light sparring

### Bag work — 1 round of 4 minutes

- Freestyle work

### Finisher

- 1 set of: push-ups, squats, hanging leg raises, superman raises, pull-ups, neck (yes, no, maybe)

### Cool-down

Finish the session with the cool-down routine (linked in the PDF).

## Boxing workout 8

### Dynamic warm-up

- Frontal stance: move at one spot and throw punches with a step forward/backward/to the sides. 4 rounds of 2 minutes
- Fighting stance: move at one spot and throw punches with a step forward/backward/to the sides. 4 rounds of 2 minutes
- 20 skater hops; 10 shoulder touches; 10 run-outs

### Shadow boxing

| Drill | Work | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frontal stance: box moving forward and backward | 2 rounds × 2 min | 30 s between rounds |
| Frontal stance: frog jumping | 1 round × 45 s | — |
| Frontal stance: defensive drill | 1 round × 2 min | — |
| Fighting stance: pendulum step counter attack | 1 round × 2 min | — |
| Fighting stance: slip and counter | 1 round × 2 min | — |
| Fighting stance: defensive drill | 1 round × 2 min | — |

### Bag work — 10 rounds of 2 minutes

One combo per round:

1. Slip – jab – cross – hook
2. Step back – jab – cross – slip – cross
3. Slip – cross – lead hook body
4. Step back – lead hook – cross – lead hook
5. Slip – lead hook body – lead hook – cross
6. Step back – cross – lead hook – cross
7. Slip – rear hook body – lead hook – cross
8. Step back – jab – jab – cross body
9. Slip – lead uppercut – cross – lead uppercut – cross
10. Freestyle using the combos above

### Isometric holds

## Boxing workout 10

### Dynamic warm-up

- 10 frog jumps with punches; 20 side-to-side jumps; 10 pogo jumps

### Shadow boxing

| Drill | Work | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frontal stance: box moving forward, backward and freestyle | 1 round × 3 min | — |
| Fighting stance: limited freestyle work | 1 round × 5 min | — |
| Fighting stance: defensive drill | 1 round × 3 min | — |

### Virtual sparring — 6 rounds of 4 minutes

1. Only single punches
2. Lead hand freestyle + rear hand only single punches
3. Lead hand freestyle + rear hand only to the body
4. Freestyle
5. Freestyle
6. Change your stance

### Extra drills (page sidebar)

The PDF prints this additional block of drills beside the workout:

- 5 rounds of 3 minutes of shadow boxing:
  1. Only movement
  2. Only long range attacks
  3. Only close range attacks
  4. Freestyle
  5. Change the stance
- 6 rounds of 3 minutes of bag work:
  1. Only movement
  2. Only long range attacks
  3. Only close range attacks
  4. Freestyle
  5. Change the stance
  6. Conditioning bag work drill (1 round)
- Tennis ball to the wall drill. 1 round of 3 minutes
- Shadow boxing with resistance bands. 6 rounds of 1 minute with 20 seconds of rest

### Finisher

- Agility footwork drills: 5 rounds of 45 seconds with 15 seconds of rest in between
- 1 set of 80–90% of max reps, with 1 minute of rest between the exercises: knuckle push-ups, squats with a med ball throw, pull-ups, sit-ups with med ball throws
- Med ball exercises (15 reps each drill): straight punches, rotational throws, slams, rotational slams, chest pass

### Cool-down

Finish the session with the cool-down routine and stretches.

## Endurance workout 2

Start with the warm-up. Then complete **3 circuits of the 10 exercises below** — work for 1 minute, rest for 30 seconds, and take 2 minutes of rest between circuits:

1. Push-up shuttle
2. Squat with med ball throws
3. Rolls
4. Plate punches
5. Side jumps
6. Plank
7. Landmine punches
8. Boxing steps with weights
9. Wall sit
10. Barbell push-outs

Finish with the stretch series.
