# Competitive Plan

The Competitive Workout Plan is the advanced plan of the KO Boxing Package by Andrii Khotin. It follows an old-school Ukrainian competitive boxing methodology and is written for boxers who already know the fundamentals and want to prepare for competition. Each week packs in 8 sessions: 5 boxing workouts plus 3 conditioning sessions (a lift, an active recovery workout and an endurance workout), followed by a full rest day.

The plan is organized into 5 weeks, each opening with a "Main focus" phase note. Week 1 is the **general preparation phase** (foundation building), weeks 2–4 are the **specialized preparation phase** (skill and conditioning optimization), and week 5 is the **peak performance phase** (fight readiness). Boxing workouts are numbered continuously from 1 to 25 across the weeks; every week also carries a daily to-do checklist and a daily stretch routine — see [Recovery and nutrition](/competitive-plan/recovery-and-nutrition).

The final rest day (day 35) closes with the instruction "Restart program with better technique!" — the 5-week block is meant to be cycled again as your technique improves. After finishing, the author suggests testing your skills with sparring or freestyle shadow boxing/bag work and sharing your results (contact: akhotinbxng@gmail.com, www.koboxingpackage.com).
