## The program at a glance

- **2 plans** — Beginner training and Competitive training. Pick one; the lessons and tips serve both.
- **8 workouts per week** — 5 boxing + 3 conditioning for maximum performance improvement. If you can't complete all sessions, adjust the schedule while maintaining consistency.
- **Strength & conditioning included** — weightlifting plans, running workouts, and a circuit workout alongside the boxing sessions.
