# Before you begin

Before beginning the program, it's highly recommended to watch the [video lessons covering boxing basics](/lessons). They give you a solid understanding of the core techniques — movement, offense, and defense.

Take your time to absorb these lessons and practice your form, especially if you're new to boxing. Skipping this step can lead to confusion and difficulty following the program, so it's worth dedicating a few days or even weeks to learning and practicing the fundamentals first.

## Which plan is for you?

- **[Basic Plan](/basic-plan)** — for those just getting started or with basic experience.
- **[Competitive Plan](/competitive-plan)** — for those who know the fundamentals and are ready to accelerate their progress. It follows an old-school Ukrainian boxing style, proven to develop high-level fighters.

Whichever you choose, read [How to use the program](/getting-started/how-to-use) first, and keep the [training tips](/training-tips) close while you work through the weeks.
