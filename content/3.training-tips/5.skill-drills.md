# Skill drill tips

- **Focus on technique** — Pay close attention to your form and execution of each drill. Proper technique is essential for improving effectively.
- **Start slowly** — Begin at a slower pace to ensure you perform the movements correctly. As you get comfortable, gradually increase speed and intensity.
- **Stay relaxed** — Avoid tensing up. Staying loose helps you move more fluidly and efficiently.
- **Repetition is key** — Practice each drill repeatedly to reinforce muscle memory and coordination for specific movements.
- **Break down movements** — If a drill involves a complex combination or footwork sequence, practice each element separately before putting them together.
- **Visualize your opponent** — During the drills, imagine facing an opponent. Visualize how you would respond and react in a real fight.
- **Stay patient** — Skill-specific drills take time to master. Keep practicing consistently.
- **Raise the difficulty** — As you become more proficient in a drill, add speed and power. Record yourself to check your form.
- **Stay engaged** — Keep your mind on the task and avoid distractions to make the most of your training time.

::callout{kind="info"}
Drills take time to master — it won't be perfect the first time around!
::
