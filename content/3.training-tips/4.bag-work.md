# Bag work tips

- **Maintain proper form** — Keep your hands up, elbows in, and chin down. Use correct footwork and maintain a good stance throughout.
- **Mix up your combos** — Practice throwing jabs, straights, hooks, and uppercuts in different sequences to improve your fluency.
- **Work on speed and power** — Alternate between fast, light punches for speed and quickness, and powerful, explosive punches to develop strength and power.
- **Move around the bag** — Don't stand still. Move, pivot, and circle the bag to simulate real fight scenarios and improve your footwork.
- **Focus on accuracy** — Aim your punches at specific spots on the bag. This improves your targeting during a match.
- **Visualize an opponent** — Imagine the bag as your opponent: work defensive movements — slipping, ducking, blocking — while counterattacking with well-timed punches.
- **Control breathing** — Proper breathing technique keeps you relaxed and maintains energy throughout the session.
- **Incorporate defense** — Practice parrying, blocking, and evasive techniques between punches.
- **Interval training** — Mix high-intensity bursts of punches with short rest intervals to simulate the intensity of a real fight and build conditioning.
- **Stay relaxed and composed** — Avoid tensing up or overexerting yourself; it preserves form and prevents unnecessary fatigue.

::callout{kind="tip"}
Adding extra bag work to this routine is never a bad idea.
::
